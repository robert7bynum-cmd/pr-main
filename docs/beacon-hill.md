# Beacon Hill Golf Club — demo profile

Pulled from https://beaconhillgolfva.com on 2026-09-03 for the 11 Sep demo build.

## Club
- **Name:** Beacon Hill Golf Club
- **Location:** Leesburg, Virginia (west of Leesburg, Loudoun County)
- **Course:** 18 holes selected from the original 27; par 72, ~7,200 yards from the tips,
  six sets of tees including a family tee
- **Architects:** originally Johnny Miller (opened 2003); redesign by Tom Clark
- **Operator:** Resort Development Partners (RDP)
- **Character:** 400-foot elevation changes, Blue Ridge Mountain views, recently reopened
  after a major renovation
- **Amenities:** clubhouse, planned par-3 course; community association membership model

## Brand
- **Primary:** gold `#E2AF47` (rgb 226 175 71)
- **Neutrals:** black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF`, charcoal `#36393D`
- **Mark:** yellow horse — `BH-YellowHorse.webp`
- **Type:** `area-extended` (headings), `astoria` (accents) — both Adobe Fonts, so the
  demo uses a close free pairing rather than licensing them
- **Tone:** understated, photographic, lots of white space

## Demo seed implications
- 18 holes, plus: practice range, clubhouse, cart barn, halfway house, two restrooms
- Departments: maintenance, cart fleet, pro shop, pace of play, F&B, caddie, management
- A club this size runs a small crew — seed ~8 staff, not 40
- Elevation changes and a new course mean irrigation and cart-path issues are
  plausible recurring categories for the seeded history
